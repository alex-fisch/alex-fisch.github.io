Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Fischer Sales Solutions LLC and DialLinePlus ("we", "us") collect, use and protect information in DialLine+ and our text messaging program. It is part of our Terms of Service.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email, phone number, NPN, role, team and sign-in activity.
  • Billing information: your subscription, phone-number and credit purchases. Card details are collected and stored by our payment processor, Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Your Data: leads and clients you or your manager import or enter, such as names, phone numbers, addresses, dates of birth, health answers, notes, policy details and, if you enter them, Social Security and bank account numbers.
  • Communications data: call records (numbers, times, durations, outcomes), text messages you send and receive, voicemail recordings, and opt-out requests.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser information and an audit log of sensitive actions (for example viewing an SSN).
  • People who receive our text messages: mobile phone number, name, the date, time and source of your opt-in consent, the messages we send you, your replies, and opt-out (STOP) and help requests.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure DialLine+: to place and route your calls and texts, show your leads and results, calculate usage and commissions, bill you, provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, keep audit records, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information and do not use Your Data to market to your leads. For people who receive our texts, we use your phone number and opt-in record only to send the messages you agreed to receive, respond to your replies, honor opt-out requests and keep required consent records.

3. Your role and your leads' information

For the lead and client information you put into DialLine+, you are responsible for having a lawful basis and any required consent to collect, use and contact those people, and for honoring their requests (such as opt-outs). We process that information on your behalf to provide the service.

4. Who we share information with

  • Service providers that operate the service for us, such as Twilio (calls, texts, phone numbers), Stripe (payments) and our hosting provider (Amazon Web Services). They may process information only to provide their services.
  • Your team: if you are on a manager's team, your manager and administrators can see your activity, results and credit balance, and can upload leads to your account. Administrators can see all accounts.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights, users or the public.
  • Business transfers: if the business is sold or reorganized, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

5. SMS and mobile information

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data and consent records collected through our text messaging program are used only to send the messages you agreed to receive and to provide customer care. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information may be shared only with service providers that deliver our messages (such as Twilio and mobile carriers), and only to provide the service. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP; reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

6. Security

Social Security and bank account numbers are encrypted when stored and are masked on screen; viewing them is logged. Connections use HTTPS. Access is limited by roles. No system is perfectly secure, and you are responsible for keeping your password safe and for the devices you use.

7. Retention

We keep information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. Backups are kept for a limited time. You can ask us to delete your account.

8. Your choices

You can update your account information in Settings, and you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting us. Some records (such as billing, audit and do-not-call records) may need to be kept.

9. Children

DialLine+ is for licensed professionals and is not directed to anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. When we make changes you will be asked to accept the new version before continuing to use DialLine+.
