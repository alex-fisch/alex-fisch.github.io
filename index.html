Features

Everything an agent needs in one place.

📞

Multi-line power dialer

Dials several leads at once and connects the first person who answers. An abandon-rate governor automatically reduces lines to stay under the FTC’s 3% limit.

🖱️

Click-to-dial browser phone

Call any lead from the browser with mute, keypad and one-click hang up. Incoming calls ring in the app with the caller’s name and file.

📍

Local numbers & number health

Agents use their own registered local numbers. Answer rates are monitored so weak numbers can be replaced before they’re labeled as spam.

📨

Two-way texting

Text conversations with each lead, plus follow-up sequences that stop automatically the moment someone replies or texts STOP.

🎙️

Calls & voicemail

See every incoming call, missed calls and voicemails, play messages in the app and call back in one click.

🩺

Health screen & best fit

A short health questionnaire suggests which carriers and products a client is likely to qualify for.

📒

Book of business

Policies, commissions from the carrier comp grid, advances, as-earned and renewal income in one view.

👥

Teams & overrides

Managers see their team live, leaderboards, and the override they earn on each deal.

↩️

Chargeback tracking

Charged-back policies automatically become debt by carrier for the agent and their upline, with repayments tracked.

TCPA & compliance

DialLinePlus is designed around the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), the FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule, carrier messaging rules and state calling laws.

Consent & opt-out

  • Texts go only to people who gave prior express written consent. For website requests, the exact consent wording, date, time and IP address are recorded with the lead.
  • STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT opt a person out automatically and permanently stop all automated texts.
  • “Not interested” replies stop follow-ups; Do Not Call requests are honored and logged.
  • Our message templates identify Fischer Sales Solutions LLC / DialLinePlus, and the first message includes STOP and HELP instructions.
  • Business texts are sent only from phone numbers in our A2P 10DLC-registered messaging campaign.

Calling & texting safeguards

  • Calls only between 8 AM and 9 PM in the lead’s own time zone, plus stricter state rules, weekends and federal holidays.
  • Texts only go out during daytime hours (9 AM – 8 PM) in the lead’s time zone.
  • Limits on how many times a person can be called in 24 hours.
  • Automatic abandon-rate governor keeps abandoned calls under 3%, with the required recorded message.
  • Leads marked Do Not Call or bad number are never dialed or texted again.
  • Every agent must accept our Terms, which require following the TCPA, TSR and do-not-call rules and scrubbing against the National Do Not Call Registry.
